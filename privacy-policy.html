PickDish Privacy Policy

Effective September 25, 2026

The short version
  • PickDish has no accounts. You never give us your name, email, or phone number.
  • Your saved dishes, ingredients, and food preferences stay on your phone.
  • We collect anonymous usage statistics through Google Analytics for Firebase to learn which features people use.
  • We don't show ads, sell your data, or track you across other apps and websites.

This policy explains what information the PickDish iPhone app ("PickDish", "we", "us") handles and why. If you have a question that isn't answered here, contact us using the details at the end.

Information that stays on your device

PickDish stores the following only on your iPhone, using the device's local storage. We don't receive a copy of it:

  • Ingredients you enter and your pantry items
  • Dishes you save
  • Your food preferences, such as "No pork" or "Peanuts" (these guide suggestions and are never sent to us)
  • The dishes you finish cooking
  • Your answers during first-time setup, and app settings such as sound effects

Deleting PickDish from your iPhone removes all of this information.

Anonymous usage analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase, a service provided by Google, to understand how the app is used so we can improve it. When you use PickDish, the app records events such as:

  • Opening the app and finishing first-time setup
  • Using "Pick something for me" or "Use what I have"
  • Viewing, saving, or cooking a dish, including which dish (its catalog number and name) and step progress
  • How many ingredients you entered (the count only, not the ingredients themselves)

Firebase also collects some information automatically, including an app-specific identifier created on install, your device model, iOS version, app version, language, and approximate country or region based on your IP address.

We never attach your food preferences, the ingredients you type, or anything that identifies you by name to these events. Analytics data is retained according to our Google Analytics data retention settings and is used only in aggregate. Google's handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, PickDish uses them only to tell you when a cooking timer finishes. These notifications are scheduled on your iPhone itself; no information is sent to us to deliver them. You can turn notifications off anytime in the iOS Settings app.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your information.
  • We don't use advertising or advertising identifiers.
  • We don't track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.
  • We don't access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or precise location.

Your choices

  • Change or clear your food preferences anytime in PickDish's Settings.
  • Delete the app to remove everything PickDish stored on your device.
  • Turn off notifications in the iOS Settings app.

Because we don't know who you are, we usually can't match analytics data to a specific person. If you have a request about your data, contact us and we'll do our best to help.

Children

PickDish is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the effective date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
