Frequently asked questions

How does “Pick something for me” work?

Tap it on Home and PickDish suggests one dish that suits the time of day and skips anything you chose to avoid. Not feeling it? Swipe or tap “Skip” for another, or tap the dish to start cooking.

How does “Use what I have” work?

Tap the ingredients you have, or search for them in English or Tagalog (for example “sibuyas” or “talong”), then tap “Show matching dishes”. “Ready to cook” dishes use only what you picked, assuming you have water, salt and cooking oil. “Need a few extras” lists what’s missing. On a dish, anything you don’t have is marked “Need”.

My cooking timer didn’t alert me
  • Make sure notifications are on: Settings › Notifications › PickDish. PickDish asks the first time you start a timer.
  • A Focus mode (like Do Not Disturb or Sleep) can hold notifications back. Allow PickDish in that Focus, or check the Lock Screen.
  • With PickDish open, the alarm rings even when your iPhone is on silent. When PickDish is in the background, the alert follows your ringer volume and silent switch.
I don’t see my timer on the Lock Screen or Dynamic Island

Turn on Settings › PickDish › Live Activities. The countdown appears once a step timer is running and you leave the app. The Dynamic Island shows it on iPhones that have one, and the Lock Screen on all supported iPhones.

How do I change what I avoid, or set vegetarian or vegan?

Tap the gear on Home to open Preferences. Choose how you eat (No preference, Vegetarian or Vegan) and what you avoid (Peanuts, Shellfish, Dairy, Gluten, Pork or Seafood). Suggestions update right away.

Is PickDish safe to use for food allergies?

PickDish’s filters are a helpful guide, not a medical check. They match dishes by their listed ingredients and can miss hidden or cross-contact allergens. If you have an allergy, always check recipe ingredients and product labels yourself.

How do I reset or delete my data?

Your ingredients, saved dishes and preferences are stored only on your iPhone. Change preferences anytime in Preferences, remove a saved dish by tapping its heart, or delete the app to erase everything PickDish stored.
